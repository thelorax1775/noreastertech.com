Rook by Nor'easter Tech

Your network, protected and looked after.

Rook sits between the internet and everything in your home or office. It blocks known threats and trackers on every device, keeps guests and smart gadgets on their own networks, and asks you about any new device that joins. We install it, watch over it and keep it up to date.

  • Protection for every device, including smart TVs and gadgets
  • Separate networks for family, guests, smart home and cameras
  • Intrusion detection that watches for known attacks, around the clock
  • Your browsing stays private, on the Rook box in your home
