What Rook does

Complete protection for your network.

Rook sits between your internet connection and everything in your home or office.

Blocks threats and trackers

Known malware, phishing, ad and tracker sites are blocked for every device, including ones that can't run security software.

Separate networks

Family, guests, smart-home gadgets and cameras each get their own network, so a guest's phone or a cheap smart plug can never reach your computers.

See every device

A clear list of everything connected, and an alert the moment something new joins.

Intrusion detection

A managed firewall and intrusion detection watch for known attack patterns around the clock.

No sneaking around the filter

Smart TVs and streaming sticks that try to bypass your settings are brought back under protection automatically.

Optional VPN

Route chosen devices through a private VPN for extra privacy on the devices you pick.

Monitored and maintained

We keep an eye on Rook's health, apply updates and fix most issues remotely, without a visit.

Simple dashboard

A plain-English view of your devices, networks and protection, right on your home network.

Safe changes

Every change is checked before it goes live and can be rolled back instantly.

Privacy

Your data stays in your home.

Your browsing history and lookups stay on the Rook box in your home. Our monitoring sees whether Rook is healthy and summaries of security events, never the sites you visit.

  • Browsing data never leaves your home
  • No ads, no data selling, ever
  • Equipment provided and maintained while you subscribe
  • Cancel any time and we put your old setup back

Questions

Rook FAQ

Do I need new internet service?

No. Rook works with your existing internet provider.

Will it slow down my internet?

Rook is built to handle typical home and small-office internet speeds without getting in the way.

Is it the same as antivirus?

Rook protects the network that every device uses. It works alongside the security built into your computers and phones, and covers the devices that have none.

What if I cancel?

Cancel any time. We collect the equipment and put your previous setup back.
