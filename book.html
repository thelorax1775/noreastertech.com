Who it is for

  • You have a spare desktop or a used mini PC, or you are about to buy one.
  • You can copy a command into a terminal. You do not need to be a sysadmin.
  • You want a lab that is useful (files, media, backups, a few apps) and does not need constant attention.
  • You want to understand backups well enough to trust them.

Who it is not for

  • Anyone who runs data-centre systems for a living and wants advanced material. Clusters, high availability, Ceph, Ansible and Kubernetes get two short lines each.
  • Anyone looking for video. This is a written book.
  • Anyone on Proxmox VE 8 or earlier. Most of it still applies, but the book was tested on 9.2 and says so.
