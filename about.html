About Nor'easter Tech

Local tech help for homes, second homes and small offices on the Upper Cape.

Nor'easter Tech sets up and fixes Wi-Fi, networks, computers and backups, keeps an eye on seasonal homes through the winter, and installs and manages Rook, our home and office network security system. When your tech acts up, you get one local number to call and a straight answer.

How we work

  • Clear prices. Every price is listed on the services page and agreed with you before any work starts.
  • Plain English. We explain what we are doing and why, without jargon or upselling.
  • Done right the first time. Networks, Wi-Fi and backups are set up properly, tested, and left in a state you can rely on.
  • Around your schedule. Evening and weekend appointments, plus remote help for quick fixes that do not need a visit.
