Questions

Good to know

What happens on a visit?

We look at the problem, confirm the price with you, fix it and test it. Before we leave, we show you what changed.

What does "equipment extra" mean?

It means our price covers the work. If you need new equipment, we'll tell you what it costs and wait for your OK before we buy it.

Can you help remotely?

Yes. Remote help is $60/hr, billed in half hours, over a secure connection while we talk on the phone.

When are you available?

Evenings and weekends, by appointment. Call or text to book a time that suits you.

Which towns do you cover?

Sandwich, Bourne, Falmouth, Mashpee and Barnstable. Elsewhere on the Cape? Just ask.
