What each one covers

Home visit

I come to you and work on whatever is bothering you: slow computers, printers, email, phones, smart TVs. Billed by the hour with a one-hour minimum.

Wi-Fi fix or mesh install

I find out why the signal is weak, then fix it with what you have or install a mesh system. The flat fee covers my time; any equipment is extra.

New computer setup with files moved over

I set up the new machine, install what you use and move your files and bookmarks across. Per computer.

Small office network and backup check

For a small shop or office: I look at the network, the router settings and the backups, and I test that a backup can really be restored. Up to 3 hours, then you get a short written list of what I found.

Second-home watch

$350 to install, then $25/month, cancel any time. Equipment is extra. Full details.

Rook home network

A managed security and privacy box for your home or small office. Setup plus a monthly fee. Full details and prices.

Remote help

I connect to your computer while we talk on the phone. Billed in half hours.

Questions people ask

What happens on a visit?

I come to you, look at the problem and work on it. You get a price before I start, and if a job needs equipment I tell you what and why before buying anything.

What does "equipment extra" mean?

The flat fee covers my time. Any hardware the job needs, such as a mesh system or sensors, is a separate cost that I show you first.

Can you help remotely?

Yes. Remote help is $60/hr, billed in half hours. I connect to your computer while we talk on the phone.

What do you not do?

I am not an alarm company or a security guard. For an emergency, call 911.

Which towns do you cover?

Sandwich, Bourne, Falmouth, Mashpee and Barnstable.
