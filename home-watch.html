What we install

Sensors where problems start.

Every house is different, so we plan the setup with you and quote the equipment before anything is bought.

Leak sensors

At the water heater, under a kitchen or bath sink, and at the lowest point of the house. More can be added.

Temperature sensors

In the coldest room and near water lines or the heating system, to catch a failing furnace early.

Power and internet

Know right away if the power or the internet goes out at the house.

Cameras, optional

Entrances and driveway only, with motion alerts. Never inside living spaces.

Alerts

The right alert, the moment it matters.

Alerts go to your phone through a free app and by email. We get them too, and follow up with you.

  • Water detected
  • Temperature drops below your limit (50°F unless you choose another)
  • Power or internet outage at the house
  • Motion at a camera, if you have one
  • A monthly check-in confirming everything is reporting
